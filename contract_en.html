Clauses

1. Object. The CONTRACTOR shall provide inspection, custody, preventive maintenance, repair coordination and communication services, in accordance with Annex I. The CONTRACTOR is not a property manager, rental agent, or legal representative.

2. Duration. The contract starts on [start date] for [months]. It automatically renews for 1 month unless either party gives 30 days' notice.

3. Price and Payment. The monthly price is [amount] € + VAT. Invoices are paid monthly by bank transfer within [days] days of receipt.

4. Scope. Services are provided with the minimum frequency specified in Annex I. Additional visits are billed according to Annex II.

5. Reports. After each visit, the CONTRACTOR will send a photo report detailing the state of the property (humidity, leaks, meter readings, pool status, etc.).

6. Repairs and Expenses. Emergency measures (shutting off water, securing a door) up to [amount] € without approval. Other repairs require the OWNER's written consent. All expenses backed by invoices.

7. Keys and Access. Handed over by inventory (Annex III). The CONTRACTOR ensures confidentiality and will not pass keys to third parties without written consent.

8. Liability. The CONTRACTOR is liable for direct damage caused by their negligence. Not liable for hidden defects, force majeure, or acts of third parties. Liability insurance is in place for [amount] €.

9. Confidentiality. The CONTRACTOR shall not disclose property data or personal data even after the contract ends (GDPR compliant).

10. Termination. Possible at any time with 30 days' notice. Early termination for non-payment (after 15 days) or serious breach.
